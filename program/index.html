Program

Workshop Program

TrustAudio will take place on Tuesday, November 10, 2026, co-located with AACL-IJCNLP 2026 in Zhuhai, China. The one-day workshop will run from 09:00 to 17:30, with three invited talks, nine oral paper presentations, and the Best Paper Award announcement.

Location: Meeting Room 4 (MR4)

Talk titles, abstracts, and biographies are available on the Speakers page.
